## 👋 Preface

<!-- CUSTOMIZE: Replace with your name and bio -->

In the last few years I took a cruise down the Snake and Columbia rivers, and came close to circumnavigating the continent by rail. When I once again got itchy feet and starting looking for my next *journey of a lifetime*, I first looked at a rail journey down the entire length of the African continent by train, but was put off when I learned formal dress was expected at dinner. Then there is a "small ship" cruise from the Canary islands to Morrocco still has a lot of appeal, and is on the back burner. and a tiny ship (eight passengers) around the coast of Scotland and the Hebrides to Denmark (way too much togetherness for my introverted personality).

But then I received a brochure from American Cruise Lines, the same company I had cruised the Columbia River with, and was captured by the idea of cruising the inland passage from Juneau, Alaska to Tacoma, Washington with stops along the way to visit eagles, bears, seals, whale, and orca. There were no single staterooms available for almost eighteen months. That was seventeen months ago. So now my bags are packed (almost), my passage paid and I --sail-- fly September 5. 🧳

Once again I will be sharing my journey with family and friends via the internet. So please join me on my latest odyssey.
